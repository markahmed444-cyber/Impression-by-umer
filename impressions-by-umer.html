FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these original fragrances or copies? +
Our fragrances are high-quality impressions inspired by world-famous luxury scents. We do not claim to sell original branded perfumes. Think of them as premium alternatives — capturing the same scent profile at a fraction of the cost, using quality fragrance oils.
How long does the scent last? +
Our fragrances typically last 6–8 hours on skin. Applying on pulse points (wrists, neck, behind ears) and on clothing extends the longevity significantly. Our Oud-based scents are particularly long-lasting.
Do you offer Cash on Delivery? +
Yes! We offer Cash on Delivery (COD) across Pakistan. You pay only when your order arrives at your doorstep. We also accept EasyPaisa and JazzCash for advance payment.
What sizes do you offer? +
We currently offer 30ml, 50ml and 100ml bottles. The 30ml is perfect for trying a new scent, while the 100ml offers the best value for your favourites. All sizes are available in our elegant black and gold bottles.
How do I place an order? +
Simply message us on WhatsApp with the fragrance name, size, and your delivery address. We'll confirm your order and dispatch it within 24 hours. Delivery typically takes 2–4 business days depending on your location.
Can I get a custom or gift set? +
Absolutely! We offer gift sets and custom bundles — perfect for weddings, Eid, or any special occasion. Message us on WhatsApp to discuss custom packaging and bulk orders.
